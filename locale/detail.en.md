# Credits & spend

See the current provider's balance, plan usage, and estimated session cost below the composer.

- **Quotas follow your model**: built-in templates for DeepSeek API key / account balances, OpenCode Go, and more, plus custom HTTP sources.
- **Session cost & TPS**: estimated spending and generation speed, with per-provider pricing and DeepSeek peak / off-peak rates.
- **Total spending**: a draggable capsule with all-time, daily, weekly, monthly, and custom date ranges.

## Get started

Open **Settings → Credits & spend → Quota queries**, choose a quota source for each provider, save, and enable it. Toggle the bottom bar, spending capsule, and TPS separately under Display.

Settings persist in the current Web / desktop profile; credentials use DSH credentials. Costs are estimates based on configured prices. Your provider's bill is authoritative.

If an update asks for a restart, fully quit and reopen DSH. For Web, restart `dsh web`.
